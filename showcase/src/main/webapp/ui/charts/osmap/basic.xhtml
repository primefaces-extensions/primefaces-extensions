OSMap Basic OSMap embeds an OpenStreetMap into your page. All you have to know is the GPS coordinate of the center of the map. Based on Leaflet by Volodymyr Agafonkin and OpenStreetMap.
Basic usage

A map needs the GPS coordinates of its center and a zoom level.

Event usage

The stateChange event fires when the map is zoomed or moved, the pointSelect event when a point is clicked. Zoom, drag or click the map.

Shape Usage

The map model can also hold shapes: polylines, polygons, circles and rectangles.
