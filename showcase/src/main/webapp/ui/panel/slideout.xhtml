SlideOut SlideOut creates tabs that slide out from the window edges to show a feedback form, contact form, live chat, etc. Tabs can be attached to the top, bottom, left or right edge, can be sticky, are easily styled with CSS and support JavaScript callbacks and Ajax behaviors for the open and close events. Based on the jQuery TabSlideOut Plugin.

Notes

A slide out tab is attached to an edge of the browser window. All demos on this page are attached to the top edge, each at a different offset so that the tabs sit next to each other below the page header. Be careful where you place the component in your own layout: the tab is positioned relative to its container, so it cannot be drawn on top of the panes of a pe:layout.

Basic Usage

SlideOut attaches nice forms to the edge of the screen. Click on the green "Contact" tab attached to the top edge of the screen. The slideout is controlled in JavaScript by its widgetVar with open(), close(), toggle() and bounce().

Multiple Tabs

Multiple slideouts can be on one page and even be opened by default: autoOpen="true" opens the orange tab after the DOM is loaded, showOn="hover" opens the red tab when you hover over it and handleOffsetReverse="true" places the handle of the blue tab on the other side of the tab.

Advanced Usage, events and callbacks

Demonstrates JavaScript callbacks (onopen, onclose), the server-side Ajax events open and close (p:ajax), and a different handle style for the open state via the ui-slideouttab-open style class. Open and close the pink "Feedback" tab.
