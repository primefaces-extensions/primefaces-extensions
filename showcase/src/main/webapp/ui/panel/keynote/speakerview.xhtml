Keynote Speaker View The speaker view presents per-slide notes in a separate browser window, together with a preview of the next slide. Presentations are made with reveal.js.
Speaker View

showNotes="true" enables the notes, which are set per slide by the note attribute of pe:keynoteItem. The notes window also gives a preview of the next upcoming slide, so it may be helpful even without notes. Press S while the presentation has the focus to open it.
